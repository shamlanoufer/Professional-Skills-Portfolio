Personal Reflection

My Professional
Learning Journey

I am Shamla Noufer, a second year undergraduate at SLIIT Kandy Uni with a strong interest in building a successful career in the IT industry. The Professional Skills module broadened my understanding of professional success, showing me that it depends not only on technical expertise but also on essential skills such as communication, teamwork, leadership, emotional intelligence, and career readiness.

Throughout the semester, I participated in a variety of sessions led by lecturers and industry professionals, gaining valuable insights into workplace expectations and professional development. This portfolio reflects my learning journey, highlighting the knowledge, skills, and experiences that have contributed to my personal and professional growth as I prepare for future opportunities in the IT industry.

Summary & Outlook

Reflection & Outlook

Overview

This portfolio reflects my learning journey across nine sessions of the Professional Skills module, covering key areas such as communication, teamwork, emotional intelligence, research skills, CV writing, meeting management, negotiation, and leadership. Through these sessions, I was able to develop a deeper understanding of essential employability skills and how they are applied in real professional and workplace environments.

Key Insights

I realized that professional skills are deeply interconnected and work together to shape overall performance in a professional environment. Effective teamwork depends on strong communication and emotional intelligence, while skills such as CV writing, research, and portfolio development enhance self-presentation, analytical thinking, and the ability to present ideas clearly and confidently.

Skills I Will Apply

BATNA & ZOPA – Understanding negotiation boundaries before making decisions
Tuckman’s Model – Identifying and adapting to team development stages
Emotional Awareness – Managing reactions and responding professionally
CV Development – Creating clear, tailored, ATS-friendly applications
Meeting Skills – Using agendas, structure, and proper documentation

Moving Forward

I will continue applying these skills in my academic work, group projects, and future career opportunities. This module has strengthened my confidence, improved my professional mindset, and encouraged me to take greater responsibility in my personal and academic growth as I prepare for a future career in the IT industry.
