Personal Reflection

My Professional
Learning Journey

I am Shamla Noufer, a second year undergraduate at SLIIT Kandy Uni with a strong interest in building a successful career in the IT industry. Before undertaking the Professional Skills module, I primarily associated career success with academic performance and technical expertise. However, this learning experience broadened my perspective and helped me understand that professional success also depends on a wide range of employability skills, including effective communication, leadership, emotional intelligence, networking, research capabilities, teamwork, and career readiness.

Throughout the semester, I had the opportunity to participate in a series of engaging sessions conducted by experienced lecturers and industry professionals. These sessions covered diverse topics such as portfolio development, professional networking through LinkedIn, CV writing, emotional intelligence, research skills, meeting management, negotiation techniques, and team leadership. Each session provided practical insights and valuable lessons that enhanced my understanding of workplace expectations and professional growth.

This portfolio serves as a reflection of my learning journey throughout the module. It highlights the knowledge I gained, the skills I developed, and the ways in which these experiences have shaped my personal and professional development. More than an academic requirement, this portfolio represents my commitment to lifelong learning and my preparation for future opportunities, including internships and a successful career in the IT industry.

Summary & Outlook

Conclusion

This portfolio documents my learning journey throughout the Professional Skills module, covering key areas such as portfolio development, professional communication, teamwork, emotional intelligence, research skills, CV writing, meeting management, negotiation, and leadership. Each session contributed to building a stronger understanding of both academic concepts and their real-world application in professional environments.

Across the weeks, I learned that professional skills are not isolated topics but interconnected abilities that shape how individuals work, communicate, and grow within teams and organizations. Skills such as negotiation, teamwork, and emotional intelligence highlighted the importance of collaboration, while CV writing, research, and portfolio development emphasized the need for clear self-presentation and critical thinking.

The practical activities, including role-plays, group tasks, and reflective exercises, helped me connect theory with experience. These activities improved my confidence in applying concepts such as BATNA, Tuckman's stages of team development, and effective communication strategies in realistic situations.

Overall, this module has helped me develop both self-awareness and professional readiness. I now understand the importance of continuous improvement, reflective practice, and adaptability in achieving long-term career success. I will continue to apply these skills in my academic work, group projects, and future career opportunities, ensuring that I grow not only as a student but also as a responsible and effective professional.
